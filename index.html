01 / The gap

A practical attack must obey the memory.

Software-only studies often choose whichever model bits are most useful to flip. Physical Rowhammer faults are rare, device-specific, directional, and tied to particular DRAM locations. ROBBIN starts from those constraints.

01

Profile

Measure stable fault patterns on the target DRAM at higher hammering intensity.

02

Score

Rank model pages by how useful the available 0→1 and 1→0 flips would be.

03

Map

Pair high-value DNN pages with physically vulnerable DRAM pages.

04

Inject

Iteratively select pages while balancing triggered behavior and benign accuracy.
